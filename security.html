Security

We take the security of your data and your account seriously. Here is how Tervexa keeps things safe.

Encrypted connections

All traffic between your device and Tervexa's servers is encrypted in transit. Sensitive data, including fault report details and images, is protected using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

Password protection

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Tervexa uses bcrypt, an industry-standard one-way hashing algorithm, to store passwords securely — even we cannot see your actual password.

Account protection

Login attempts are rate-limited to protect your account against automated guessing attacks. We recommend a unique password of at least 8 characters, including both letters and numbers, that you do not reuse on other sites.

Session security

When you log in, Tervexa creates a secure session tied to your account. Using "Remember me" keeps you signed in on that device; logging out or clearing your browser data ends the session.

Offline data

Fault report data stored temporarily on your device while offline is encrypted, and is only synced to our servers once you are back online. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in Tervexa, please contact us directly at luckyvictor3@gmail.com so we can investigate and address it promptly. Please do not disclose the issue publicly until we have had a chance to resolve it.
