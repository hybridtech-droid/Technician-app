Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

iTechAssist(iTA) collects only the personal data necessary to provide the service. We do not sell your data to any third party.

What we collect

Name, email address, phone number, company or organizationname, fault reports and upload images you submit through the platform.

Why we collect it

To provide AI-powered fault diagnosis, maintain your account and improve the service.

How we protect it

All data is stored securely and encrypted. Images are stored on secure cloud servers

Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at luckyvictor3@gmail.com

Local device storage

When operating in offline mode, iTechAssist temporarily stores fault report data including text descriptions and images on your device using browser local storage technology. This data remains private to your device and is only transmitted to iTechAssist servers when you have an active internet connection. We do not access locally stored data remotely.
