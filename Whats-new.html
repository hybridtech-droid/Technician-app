What's New

v1.1.0 — Latest release

Here's what's included in Tervexa, most recent update first. We'll keep this page updated as new features and languages roll out.

New in v1.1.0

Roles now shape what you can do

Your account role determines what you can see and do in the fault log — submit reports, manage every report, or both, depending on whether you're a technician, engineer, supervisor, manager or administrator.

New: an admin panel for managing accounts

Administrators can now view every account, change someone's role, or deactivate a login — all from one screen.

Export your fault log

Download the fault log as CSV, Excel or PDF, or print it directly — available to engineers, supervisors, managers and administrators.

v1.0.0 — Initial release

AI-powered fault diagnosis

Describe your issue or upload a photo and get an instant, step-by-step diagnosis to guide your troubleshooting.

Four request types

Report a fault, log an installation and commissioning job, request after-sales support, or get help with an application or process issue — all from one form.

Fault log and tracking

Every report you submit is saved with a status — open, in progress or resolved — plus root-cause tracking and resolution notes.

Works offline

No internet? No problem. Fault reports are stored on your device and sync automatically once you're back online.

Multi-language support

Use Tervexa in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese or Swahili, with AI diagnosis delivered in the same language.

Spare parts links

Diagnosis results include direct links to verified spare parts suppliers, so you can order what you need right away.

Ask AI

A dedicated chat for quick equipment questions that don't need a full report.

Role-based accounts

Sign up as a technician, application specialist, engineer, supervisor, manager or administrator — Tervexa adapts to your role.

More features and languages are on the way. Check back here for updates.
