About this app

Tervexa is built for field technicians and engineers who need fast, accurate fault diagnosis on the job.

Using AI, the app analyses fault descriptions and images submitted by technicians and returns step-by-step troubleshooting guidance in real time.

Multi-language Support

Tervexa is designed for a global workforce. The platform currently supports the following languages with more being added regularly:

  • English
  • French - Français
  • Spanish - Español
  • German - Deutsch
  • Portuguese - Português
  • Arabic - العربية
  • Chinese - 中文
  • Hindi - हिन्दी
  • Swahili - Kiswahili
  • Russian - Русский
  • Japanese - 日本語
  • Korean - 한국어
  • Swedish - Svenska

The AI-powered Technical intelligence engine responds in the same language the user uses - no translation required. Field engineers across Africa, Europe, Middle East, Asia and beyond can use Tervexa in their native language.

  • Software and control engineer
  • Structural and civil engineers

Where we operate

Tervexa is currently operating in Nigeria with active expansion into West Africa underway. Our roadmap includes plans to expand to other regions and globally.

Built with global architecture, multiple language support, local and international spare parts integration and region-specific compliance frameworks are core to our platform design.

Works offline

No internet? No problem. Technicians can log fault reports, upload photos and record observations without any connection. The app stores everything locally and syncs automatically the moment connectivity is restored — no data is ever lost.
